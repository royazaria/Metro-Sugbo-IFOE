What to Know

Cebu City — exact venue confirmed once finalized. Centrally accessible by jeep, taxi, and Grab. Parking available at Courtside and VIP rates.

⏰

Doors open 90 minutes before tip-off. National Anthem & opening ceremony on Weekend 1, Saturday. Latecomers welcome — just don't miss the first quarter.

Common Questions

Ticket FAQs

How do I pay?

Online payment options (GCash, PayMongo, credit card) are coming soon. For now, when you check out we'll connect you with our team via Messenger to confirm your seats and arrange payment by bank transfer or in-person reservation. Your seats are held for 24 hours.

Are these prices per game or per weekend?

Per weekend. One ticket covers all the games scheduled for that weekend (Saturday + Sunday). Buying all three weekends? Message us — we'll do a 3-weekend bundle deal.

Can I bring my kids?

Absolutely — this is a family event. Children under 5 are free if seated on a parent's lap. Otherwise, kids ages 5+ need their own ticket (any tier).

What if I can't attend after buying?

Tickets are transferable. Send us the new attendee's name and we'll re-issue. No refunds, but you can also gift your seat to a friend or donate it to one of our community guests.

Will there be food and drinks?

Yes. Local vendors will be on-site for food and drinks at all weekends. VIP and Courtside guests get welcome drinks and light snacks included; Courtside has full F&B service at your seat.

Is there a livestream if I can't make it?

Yes — we'll livestream all games on the official Metro Sugbu Eagles Facebook page. But trust us: this one's better in person.

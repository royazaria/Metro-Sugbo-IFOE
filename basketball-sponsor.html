Why The Eagles Cup

A Court. A Cause. A Crowd That Buys.

This isn't a generic basketball league. The Eagles Cup is a three-weekend hardcourt event hosted by the Metro Sugbu Eagles — a fraternity of Cebu's professionals, business owners, and civic leaders.

Every peso raised goes back to community outreach: feeding programs, disaster relief, scholarships. So when you sponsor the Cup, you're not just buying signage. You're buying brand alignment with brotherhood, service, and Cebuano pride.

Common Questions

Sponsor FAQs

When does the tournament happen?

The Eagles Invitational Cup is scheduled for Q3 2026, played across three weekends in Cebu City. Final dates and venue lock in once title sponsorship is confirmed — book early and you'll have a say in the date.

Can we pay in installments?

Yes. We're flexible — most of our larger sponsors split payments across two or three milestones (e.g. signing, opening night, finals). Talk to us about what works for your finance team.

Do you accept in-kind sponsorships?

Absolutely. Beverages, gear, hospitality, transport, F&B for players and staff, prize merchandise — anything that takes a line item off our budget. We value in-kind partnerships and will tier them accordingly.

Where exactly does the money go?

Sponsorship funds operating costs first (venue, refs, equipment, livestream production, security, prizes). All net proceeds after costs go directly to Eagles community outreach: relief operations, scholarships, and feeding programs. We publish a post-event report.

What if my brand wants exclusivity in a category?

Title sponsors get full category exclusivity. Gold and Silver sponsors can request category exclusivity as a custom add-on — we'll work it out so your competitor isn't on the same banner.

Can we sponsor a specific game or weekend instead of the full tournament?

Yes. Game-day sponsorships and championship-night-only packages are available. Great for brands who want to test the waters before going bigger next year.

Ready to Stand Beside the Eagles?

One conversation. We'll send you the deck, walk you through the tiers, and figure out the right fit for your brand. No pressure — just a call.

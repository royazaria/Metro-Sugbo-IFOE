Who We Are

Eagles Fly Together

Metro Sugbu is a proud chapter of the International Fraternal Order of Eagles (IFOE) — one of the world's most respected fraternal organizations, now soaring across the Philippines with purpose and pride.

We are a community of men and women bound by a shared commitment to service, brotherhood, and the upliftment of our society. Based in the heart of Cebu, we bring together professionals, leaders, and passionate individuals who believe in making a difference.

Our motto — "Deo et Patria" (God and Country) — is not just words. It is the way we live, lead, and love.

Eagles In The Community

Reaching the Heights of Leadership

Metro Sugbu Eagles doesn't just talk about community — we live it. We are honored to welcome Cebu City Councilor, Police Brigadier General, Paul Labra II as our Kuya, a brother in our fraternity.

This is a testament to what the Eagles represent: people of integrity, influence, and genuine care for their city choosing to stand with us.

Why We Sing

One Stage. Many Bands. Every Peso for the Cause.

Soar High is the Metro Sugbu Eagles' answer to a simple question: what if Cebu's best musicians and Cebu's most generous crowd showed up in the same room?

One night. Local bands across genres. A room full of people who came because the music's good — and stayed because the cause is real. 100% of net proceeds go to our community outreach beneficiary, distributed transparently after the show.

So whether you come for the riffs, the ballads, or the brotherhood — you're part of something bigger than the gig.

Common Questions

Show FAQs

When is the show?

Soar High is scheduled for the second half of 2026 — exact date locks once the venue is finalized. Subscribe to the newsletter for the official announcement first.

How exactly do "100% of proceeds" work?

Sponsors and Eagles members cover production costs (venue, sound, security, marketing). That means every peso from ticket sales goes directly to the chosen beneficiary. We publish a transparent breakdown post-event.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes — this is an all-ages show. Children 5 and under are free if they can be seated with you. Kids 6+ need their own ticket.

How do I pay?

Online payment options (GCash, PayMongo, card) are coming soon. For now, complete the form and we'll DM you via Messenger to confirm payment by bank transfer or in-person reservation. Spot held for 24 hours.

I'm a sound engineer / lighting tech / videographer — can I help?

Absolutely. We always need pros willing to volunteer or work at cost. Message us with your role and rate.

Will there be a livestream?

Yes — selected sets will be streamed on the Metro Sugbu Eagles Facebook page so people who can't make it can still tune in (and donate). But the room is where the energy lives.
